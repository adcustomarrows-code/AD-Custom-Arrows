BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open script.js.
2. Replace PRICING.shaft values with your actual dealer/material costs.
3. Verify labor and upgrade pricing.
4. Replace GOOGLE_FORM_URL with your Google Form.
5. Add your real product/component prices as you establish dealer accounts.

IMPORTANT
The displayed price is an ESTIMATE. A&D should verify shaft availability, spine, finished length, component compatibility, and final price before accepting payment.

GOOGLE FORM
The current button opens your Google Form. To make the quote builder automatically pass the selected configuration into Google Form fields, the next step is to map your Google Form's pre-filled entry IDs to the builder fields.

PUBLISHING
Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js and hero.jpg to your web host.
